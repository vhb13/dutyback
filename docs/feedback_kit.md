# Real-user feedback kit (15 minutes)

Use this when you show DutyBack to a real small importer, a customs broker, or someone who handles a small business's imports. Only what they actually say goes into the report. If you haven't had a conversation yet, the report says "first feedback pending."

## Before you meet
- Have the app running (`python run.py`). In demo mode, use the recorded samples.
- Don't ask for their real paperwork. Use the sample importers. If they want to try their own PDFs later, that's their choice, on their computer.
- Ask whether you can quote them by first name, by role only ("owner of a small tile importer"), or not at all.

## Script
1. **Context (2 min).** "Did your business pay the 2025 tariffs that the Supreme Court struck down? Have you claimed refunds yet? Who handles it, you or your broker?"
2. **Show (5 min).** Run the Lotus sample. Point out:
   - the money buckets
   - the "money at risk this week" banner
   - one entry's quotes and checks
   - the action plan and broker email
   - the CAPE file and calendar
3. **Ask (6 min).**
   - "Is anything here wrong or confusing?"
   - "Which part would you actually use: the sorting, the deadlines, the broker email, or the CAPE file?"
   - "Did you know about the 80-day CAPE limit and the 180-day protest window?"
   - "Would you trust it more or less because it shows the quotes from your documents?"
   - "What's missing before you'd use it on your own entries?"
   - "Would you share it with anyone? Who?"
4. **Close (2 min).** Thank them, and ask if you can follow up.
