Thank you for the class. Building agents step by step in the homework is what made this project possible.

I've submitted my final project, DutyBack, on Canvas, and I'm attaching the report, slides and demo video here for your reference. DutyBack helps small importers claim refunds of the tariffs the Supreme Court struck down. It reads their customs paperwork, sorts every entry into the right refund route with its exact deadline, and prepares the CAPE upload file, a deadline calendar and an email to their broker. The agents read and explain; plain code checks their work and applies CBP's rules.

A note on how it runs: the agents run live on the course key (PORTKEY_API_KEY with gpt-6-luna), and the app reads the .env from its own folder or the folder above. I first developed it on Claude, and that engine is still an option. The repo is here: https://github.com/vhb13/dutyback
