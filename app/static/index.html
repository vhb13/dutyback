Not legal advice. DutyBack sorts your entries and prepares files for you and your customs broker to review. The tariff refund rules are still changing (a court class was certified on Oct 8, 2026 and an appeal is pending), so every result shows the date of the rules it used.

1. Your customs paperwork

Pick a sample importer (made-up companies, synthetic paperwork) or upload your own PDFs: CBP Form 7501 entry summaries, your broker's statement, an ACE report, or an email from your broker.

2. Working on it

    Demo mode: this replays a real recorded run (no API key found in .env and Claude Code isn't logged in). Waiting time is shortened.

    DutyBack is a student project. It is not affiliated with U.S. Customs and Border Protection and is not legal advice. Sample companies and paperwork are synthetic. Agents run on gpt-6-luna (course key) or Claude.
